# A smaller global demand bound from affine extension height

This refinement applies to the online minor compressor with its original request rule, arithmetic sign tests, scale extraction and extension theorem. Denote the dissociation cap by $D$, the requested type exponent by $K$, and put $J=K+\log_2(D+1)$. It suffices to use a uniform range with $\log F=O(D^3 J)$ in place of the published uniform $\Theta(D^4 J)$. It reduces the dependence on $D$ in the complete price tree even if the external visibility routine is left exactly as published.

## A right-hand-side bound for mixed strict and weak inequalities

Consider a feasible finite system of strict or weak affine inequalities in $k$ unknowns $x$, with an additional rational parameter vector $y=p/q$. All original row coefficients, including the constant and parameter coefficients, are integers of combined one-norm at most $A\ge2$. Suppose all $|p_i|$ and the positive integer $q$ are bounded by $U$. After substitution every constraint can be written with the same integer left matrix $C$ and a right side $b_i/q$ with $|b_i|\le AU$. There is a solution with common denominator $q|\det W|$ and absolute numerators at most $(k+1)AU(A+1)^k$, where $W$ is integral, $|\det W|\le(A+1)^{k+1}$. In particular the necessary height is linear in $U$.

To prove this, add a margin $t$ to the left of each strict row, replace it by a weak row, and add $0\le t\le1$. Choose a closed coordinate orthant containing some solution with positive margin (use $t=1$ if there are no strict rows). The orthant makes the new polyhedron pointed. Maximizing $t$ has a finite attained positive optimum at a vertex. This argument also handles empty row sets, consistent equalities, lower-dimensional feasible sets and arbitrary initial lineality. Let $W$ be a full-rank set of tight left rows in the $k+1$ variables. Each row has norm at most $A+1$. The common Cramer denominator is $q |\det W|$. In each numerator a single column of $W$ is replaced by the integer vector of tight right-hand-side numerators. Expansion along this column uses at most $k+1$ cofactors, each of magnitude at most $(A+1)^k$ by Hadamard. The orthant and margin rows have right numerator zero or $q$, also bounded by $AU$. Reversing the sign when necessary gives the asserted solution.

The linear height dependence can be needed even for the single equality $x=p/q$. The proof requires no algorithm that enumerates all unseen constraints.

## Substitution into the published compressor

In the upstream extension proof, $k=|I|<D$ coordinates on a maximal dissociated set extend the answers on a requested subset $S$. The feasibility transfer at type $M=(D+1)A^{D+1}$ already gives a solution of the type system at $y=f_S$. The norm of its original cleared integer rows is bounded by $A=2^K(D+4)/\eta$. The previous estimate multiplies all row norms by $U$ and then takes a determinant of order $D$. Keeping the coefficient matrix fixed and applying the lemma gives a common denominator $q|\det W|$ also divisible by the denominator $q$ of $f_S$. Each unqueried coordinate is an affine function $h_u(x)$ with at most $k$ coefficients in $\{-1,0,1\}$ and a constant of magnitude at most $3$ on the $\eta$-grid. With $\eta^{-1}$ integral, all the old and new coordinates have common denominator $q|\det W|/\eta$. The old numerators on this denominator are bounded by $U(A+1)^D/\eta$. The new numerators are bounded by $[D^2+3] U(A+1)^D/\eta$. Thus one may take

$$F_{\rm new}=(D+2)^2(A+1)^D U/\eta.$$

Use the same uniform maximum over $0\le p\le m<D$ and the same $U=B^{p+2}/\eta$ as before. Writing $J=K+\log_2(D+1)$ gives $\log M=\Theta(DJ)$, $\max\log C,\max\log B=\Theta(D^2J)$ and $\max\log U=\Theta(D^3J)$. The term $p=m=D-1$ attains these orders in the prescribed maximum. Therefore the *new supplied uniform bound* has $\log F_{\rm new}=\Theta(D^3J)$, as against $\Theta(D^4J)$ from the published formula. The original physical sign-test budget uses the same $M,C,\eta$ and remains valid. All range constants can be computed with short integer arithmetic at initialization. This proof changes the outer bound supplied to the price learner. It does not require the implementation to solve the existential extension LP or to materialize any unqueried minor.

For its fixed tree the count of price callbacks is proportional, up to the common dimension factors, to $\log(NF)$. The new bound therefore saves a factor $\Theta(D)$ on the prescribed complete-run schedule, independently of the graph improvement in $K$. Applying the same height improvement to an ordered baseline maintains the earlier graph-versus-triangular relative comparison. The upstream counting proof already permits $D=O(N^2\log(N+1))$ for dense inputs. Combining this cap with a refreshed graph gives $\log R=O(N^6\log^4(N+1))$. With the four-slot schedule of the learner ledger this gives $O(N^{54}\log^4(N+1))$ callbacks per complete run, or $O(N^{45}\log^4(N+1))$ with the five-slot market. These are dimension-only paid price-oracle counts before charging callback contents and restarts. The five-slot market has a different cost per call.
